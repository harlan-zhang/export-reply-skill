# B2B 场景话术库

> 客户 = 经销商、改装工作室、车队、卡丁车项目方、整车厂。语气专业、有分寸、结构清晰。
> 核心动作：**先分级、再报价**。信息不全时给价值、要信息，不给数字。

## B2B 客户分级（写回复前先定级）

| 级别 | 特征 | 回复策略 |
|---|---|---|
| **A 真经销** | 有实体店/网站/社媒，明确目标市场，能说出车型与月量 | 快速给产品矩阵 + 报价清单路径，推进到报价 |
| **B 潜在大户** | 有渠道但信息模糊，问得很泛 | 给专业度建立信任，收 1—2 个关键信息 |
| **C 比价党** | 只问 "best price" 不给任何背景 | 礼貌确认 1 个信息，不投入太多 |
| **D 钓鱼/同行** | 索要完整价格表、客户名单、参数表 | 只给公开信息，不交底 |

## 场景 1：首次询盘（"请发价格表"）

**结构**：承接 → 给价值（产品矩阵一句话）→ 只问 2 个信息 → 承诺时间

```
Thanks for reaching out. We build matched battery-controller-motor systems for the Light Bee, Ultra Bee and Talaria platforms, plus the APEX controller and motor range — so the pricing depends on which platform you're selling into and the spec you need.

Two things and I can send you a proper price list: which platforms are your main volume, and roughly how many units a month?

Best regards,
[Your name] | TRB POWER
```

## 场景 3：问 OEM / 定制

**结构**：大方说能做什么 → 说明需要什么才能评估 → 不承诺 MOQ 与费用

```
We do OEM work on the battery side — housing and dimensions, discharge and charge connectors, cell mix and capacity, BMS choice and Bluetooth, display and logo, charger and charge current, plus packaging and nameplate.

What I can't quote blind is the MOQ, tooling cost and sample lead time — those depend on how far you want to move from our standard build.

Tell me which of those you actually need changed and I'll take it to engineering.

Best regards,
[Your name] | TRB POWER
```

## 场景 4：问认证（CE / UL / UN38.3）

**结构**：不笼统承诺 → 要 SKU + 目的国 → 承诺给文件清单

```
Certification is per SKU and per destination, so I don't want to give you a blanket "yes".

Which SKU and which market are you importing into? I'll pull the test and transport documents we have for that exact item and send you the list — then you'll know precisely what's covered and what isn't.

Best regards,
[Your name] | TRB POWER
```

**要点提示**：锂电运输（UN38.3 / Class 9）是 B2B 客户真正关心的，不要回避。

## B2B 通用禁忌

- ❌ 没有 5 项信息就给价格、MOQ、交期
- ❌ 承诺独家、免费样品、账期、延长保修
- ❌ 笼统说 "CE certified / UL listed"
- ❌ 主动报创作者合作价
- ❌ 说自己是 Sur-Ron / Talaria 的 OEM 或官方供应商
- ❌ 用 "We are a leading manufacturer with 20 years experience" 开场
- ❌ 报 TRBEXA R Series 的速度、交期、认证状态
- ❌ 结尾 "please feel free to contact us"
