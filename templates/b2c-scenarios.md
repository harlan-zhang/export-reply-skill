# B2C 场景话术库

> 客户 = 终端骑手、个人改装玩家。语气短、口语、直接。模板给的是**结构和节奏**，正文必须按客户原话重写。

## 场景 1：问"这个电池能装我的车吗"（最高频）

**结构**：给方向 → 要 2 个关键信息 → 承诺时间

```
Yes, the 72V pack is built around the Light Bee compartment. Two things decide the exact fit: the year of your bike and the connector on your current pack.

Send me a photo with the battery cover off and tell me the year — I'll confirm the configuration today.
```

**要点提示**：不要只回 "yes it fits"。不要问超过 2 个问题。

---

## 场景 2：问价格（B2C）

**结构**：给产品页 → 说明以页面选项为准 → 问目标配置

```
Price depends on the configuration — the 72V 45Ah starts at the page price, and the 15C cell and BMS options move it.

Here's the page with all configurations: [link]

What are you running now, and what power are you targeting? I'll point you at the right one instead of the most expensive one.
```

**要点提示**：B2C 可以直接给产品页链接，但不复述价格数字。

---

## 场景 3：问"能跑多快 / 能跑多远"

**结构**：说明变量 → 问当前配置 → 给方向

```
Top speed and range depend on the whole build — gearing, rider weight, terrain, temperature and which controller you're on. I won't give you a number that won't hold up on the trail.

What controller and motor are you running now? With that I can tell you what the pack will actually support.
```

**要点提示**：绝不承诺续航里程和极速。

---

## 场景 4：问"装完提升多少"

**结构**：给可验证的参数对比 → 落到具体配置

```
On the 72V 45Ah you're looking at 300A continuous and 650A peak — 22.2kW rated, 48.1kW pulse. That's the pack side. What you feel on the trail also depends on the controller and gearing.

What's on the bike now? Stock, or already upgraded?
```

---

## 场景 5：犹豫 / 比价（"太贵了，XX 家更便宜"）

**结构**：不贬低竞品 → 讲系统匹配 → 给降级选项

```
Fair enough — price matters. The difference on ours is that the cell spec and the BMS are what the 300A continuous rating actually rests on.

If budget is the real constraint, the 72V 50Ah is a genuine step down in peak power but still a solid upgrade. What power do you actually need? I'd rather put you on the right pack than the biggest one.
```

---

## 场景 6：问发货 / 多久到

**结构**：确认目的地 → 说明锂电运输特殊性 → 不报具体天数

```
Where are you shipping to? Lithium packs go as dangerous goods, so the route and the lead time depend on the destination and the carrier.

Send me your country and postcode and I'll get you the actual shipping options for that config.
```

---

## 场景 7：问保修

**结构**：给已确认的事实 → 说明范围需确认

```
The battery packs carry a 1-year warranty. What's covered and what isn't depends on the failure — water ingress and over-voltage from a mismatched controller are the usual grey areas.

If something's already wrong, send me photos of the BMS label and the connector and I'll take it from there.
```

---

## 场景 8：售后 / 出问题

**结构**：接情绪 → 认已知事实 → 要信息 → 给时间点

```
Sorry to hear that — I know how frustrating a dead pack is mid-season.

Send me photos of the BMS label, the connector and the display reading. I'll check the batch records against your order and come back to you by tomorrow with what I find and what we do next.
```

**要点提示**：不认责、不承诺赔偿、不谈责任划分。

---

## 场景 9：问安装 / 自己能不能装

**结构**：诚实评估难度 → 给判断依据

```
If you've swapped a pack before, this is a straightforward job — the pack drops into the stock compartment and the connectors are keyed. The part that trips people up is the BMS wiring and the controller setting for the new voltage.

What controller are you on? If it's stock, the voltage has to be handled before the pack goes in.
```

---

## 场景 10：问配件 / 单个零件

**结构**：确认型号 → 给链接或说明

```
Which pack is it for? The connectors differ between the 72V and the Ultra Bee series — I don't want to send you the wrong one.

Tell me the pack model and I'll send the exact part.
```

---

## 场景 11：客户只发了个"hi" / "price?"

**结构**：一句话破冰 + 一个具体问题

```
Hi — happy to help. Which bike and which part are you looking at? Send me the model and I'll get you the right numbers.
```

**要点提示**：不要贴一整段公司介绍。

---

## 场景 12：客户问"你们是不是 Sur-Ron 官方"

**结构**：明确否认 → 说明真实关系 → 转回能力

```
No — we're not affiliated with Sur-Ron. Our packs and controllers are built to be compatible with the Light Bee and Ultra Bee platforms, and we do our own engineering on the battery, BMS and controller side.

That's actually why the fitment questions matter — tell me your year and I'll confirm exactly what works.
```

---

## 场景 13：客户想批量买（个人买家冒出 B2B 信号）

**结构**：接住 → 转到 B2B 轨道 → 问身份

```
Sounds like you're buying for more than one bike. If you're reselling or building for customers, I can put together dealer pricing — that works differently from the retail page.

Are you a shop, or building for a group? And roughly how many units a month?
```

**要点提示**：识别到 B2B 信号立刻换轨道，但不要在 B2C 回复里直接报批发价。

---

## B2C 通用禁忌

- ❌ 一上来问公司名、月采购量、营业执照
- ❌ 一封回复里堆 5 个问题
- ❌ 贴大段公司介绍
- ❌ 承诺续航、极速、充电时间
- ❌ 未确认就写 plug-and-play
- ❌ 结尾 "let us know if you have any questions"
